Measurement question

Same-day federal-funds target changes (d_same) are a behavioral label for policy actions. They are an incomplete label for the hawkishness of FOMC *text*, especially when the Committee holds the target. This bench pre-registers gates that ask whether TypeSafe/Jev recovers easy hawk/dove orderings, tracks d_same on scheduled action days, and still separates holds from cuts on a textual score.
